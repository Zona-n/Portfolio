Google Cloud Chicago Sprinternship 2026 Team of 10

CloudPulse

A grounded AI assistant that keeps support engineers current on Google Cloud changes.

Google Cloud ships across ~300 products faster than anyone can track, and a general-purpose model answers from stale training data. CloudPulse cites a real source for every claim. Built in three weeks with a team of 10 at Google's Chicago office.

  • I owned the data layer. A BigQuery pipeline that extracted 49,446 release notes across 296 products and 16 years, HTML-stripped and chunked for import.
  • Vertex AI RAG grounding over a curated corpus, plus a Vertex AI Search data store for live official docs.
  • Unified both retrievers in one tool layer with a keyword fallback, so empty results degrade instead of hallucinating.

A semantic research dashboard for 600+ NASA bioscience publications.

NASA has run hundreds of biology experiments in space and the findings sit in papers nobody has time to read. I built the backend that ingests, embeds, and answers questions across all of them.

03
Discovery Partners Institute Summer 2025

iHEATRISK

A heat-risk model that tells people what to actually do.

Extreme heat kills more Americans than any other weather event, and public guidance is generic. This reads a person's health profile, work setting, and living conditions, then returns advice grounded in real research.

  • Read 50 research documents and categorized the interventions into 5 types.
  • No training data existed, so I generated 500 realistic user profiles with pandas, NumPy, and Faker.
  • Trained a scikit-learn Random Forest across five risk levels, served through a Flask API.
